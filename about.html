Our story

Pronto Contractors Limited is headquartered in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We serve major players in the oil & gas sector with onshore construction, offshore fabrication, steel structures, and equipment leasing. But we are not just oil and gas.

Walk through Hypercity Mall in Port Harcourt. Look up at the steel framework holding the roof. That is us. Walk through Garden City Mall — the long span steel roofing. That is us. Visit Ebocha Farm Feed Mill and OMOC Farm Feed Mill — industrial buildings where agriculture meets engineering. That is us too.

We build what Nigeria needs. Today. Tomorrow. Decade after decade. Our parent company brings over 50 years of experience and advanced engineering systems. We are proud to be the first DNV 2.7-1 certified manufacturer in Nigeria, a legacy we continue with every skid, container, and platform we fabricate.

How we actually work

Not marketing fluff. Real process.

1. Engineering & Planning

API, DNV, ABS, BS EN standards drive every drawing and calculation before welding starts.

2. Fabrication

Certified facility, certified welders, certified processes. Full material traceability.

3. Quality Control

Independent inspection, NDT, X-ray for critical welds. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't ship.

4. Installation & Commissioning

We deliver, install, test, and commission. Then stay until you sign off.
